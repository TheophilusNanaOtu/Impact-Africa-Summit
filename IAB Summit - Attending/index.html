About the Summit

The Impact Africa Business & Investment Summit London 2026 brings together business leaders, investors, policymakers and partners to shape the future of the UK–Africa relationship. Under the theme Building the UK-Africa Corridor, the event will highlight opportunities for trade, investment, innovation and collaboration across sectors that matter to both regions.

Powered by Impact Africa Business and the University of East London, it is a place to connect, exchange ideas and build partnerships that last.
